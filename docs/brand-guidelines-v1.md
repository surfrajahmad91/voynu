# VOYNU Brand Guidelines v1

## Brand direction

VOYNU is presented as a modern, trustworthy mobility and travel platform. The visual system is clean, confident, technology-forward and intentionally distinct from common ride-hailing brands.

## Gradient

Primary brand gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #00B4A6, #2563EB)`.

## Typography

Preferred family: Poppins, with Plus Jakarta Sans/system fallbacks.

## Product application

- Customer: Deep Navy app icon with Teal → Blue mark.
- Saarthi: Teal app icon with Navy mark details.
- Admin: Violet app icon with Blue/Violet mark details.
- All three remain recognizably one VOYNU family.
- Notification icons remain monochrome so Android can render them correctly.

## UI principles

- Rounded cards and controls, generally 10–24px radii.
- White surfaces on a very light cool background.
- Navy for primary headings and high-trust surfaces.
- Teal for primary action/focus states.
- Blue for secondary navigation and technology cues.
- Violet is reserved primarily for Admin identity and selected emphasis.
- Semantic green/yellow/red are reserved for status, not brand identity.

## Logo usage

Use the supplied SVG icon as the canonical small-format mark. Do not recolor or distort the mark. For notification surfaces, use the dedicated monochrome notification badge/icon rather than the full-color app icon.
